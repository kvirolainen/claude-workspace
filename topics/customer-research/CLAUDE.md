# Customer Research

A *reference* topic — not a project with a status, just knowledge that accumulates: what you've learned about customers. (Contrast with `example-project`, which is active work with a status and maybe code.)

**When to open:** any work needing customer context — interviews, segments, objections.

## Files in this topic
- `notes.md` — synthesized findings.
- `interviews/` — raw notes, one file per conversation (bulk, not indexed).
